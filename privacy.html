Alarmbo

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Alarmbo does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Alarmbo keeps the following only on your iPhone, so the app can work:

  • Your alarms and their settings (times, repeat days, missions, snooze options).
  • Sounds you choose to import. Alarmbo saves its own copy so your alarm keeps working if the original file is moved or deleted.
  • A short diagnostics log of alarm events, which you can view in the app to troubleshoot an alarm.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Alarms — lets Alarmbo schedule alarms that ring even when Silent Mode or a Focus is on.
  • Files — only used when you choose an audio file to import as an alarm sound. Alarmbo reads only the file you pick.

Third parties

Alarmbo does not include any third-party SDKs and does not share data with third parties.

Children

Because Alarmbo collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
