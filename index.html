Common questions

Will my alarm ring if my phone is on silent?
Yes. Alarmbo uses iOS's built-in alarm system, so alarms ring in Silent Mode and during a Focus, just like the Clock app.

How loud will it be?
Alarm volume follows your Ringtone and Alerts volume (Settings → Sounds & Haptics), not your media volume. If that slider is all the way down, the alarm will be quiet.

Why did my alarm ring again after I stopped it?
For mission alarms, the system Stop button only silences the alarm for a moment so you don't fall back asleep. It turns off for good when you complete the mission or snooze (within the alarm's snooze limit). If you really need to stop it, Emergency Dismiss is always available inside a mission — hold it for 5 seconds.

My alarm didn't ring.
Check that Alarms is allowed for Alarmbo in Settings → Apps → Alarmbo. If it still happens, open Settings → Diagnostics in Alarmbo and include what you see when you contact us.
